## What V7 does

V7 is V5 with the cover floor switched off for beds under 6 m2. The
question it answers: does that keep V5's gain on medium and large beds
while removing the damage V5 did to small ones?

**Small beds.** V7's largest empty patch is 0.147, worse than V5
(-) and level with V2 (0.147). With no floor and
the same seeds, a small bed under V7 should be built exactly as V2 builds
it. Checked scenario by scenario: all 5 identical to V2.

**Beds of 6 m2 or more.** V7's largest empty patch is 0.149, worse than
V5 (-) and better than V2 (0.150). On these beds V7 and V5
apply the same rule with the same seeds. Checked: 95 of 95 differ from V5 (S001, S002, S003, S004, S005, S006, S007, S008, S009, S010, S011, S012, S013, S014, S015, S016, S017, S018, S019, S020, S021, S022, S023, S024, S025, S026, S027, S028, S029, S030, S031, S032, S033, S034, S035, S036, S037, S038, S039, S040, S041, S042, S043, S044, S045, S046, S047, S048, S049, S050, S051, S052, S053, S054, S055, S056, S057, S058, S059, S060, S061, S062, S063, S064, S065, S066, S067, S068, S069, S070, S071, S072, S074, S075, S076, S078, S079, S080, S082, S083, S084, S085, S086, S087, S089, S090, S091, S092, S093, S094, S095, S096, S097, S099, S100).
So V7 is an exact splice - V2 on small beds, V5 on the rest - and its

Beds reaching their target: V2 33, V5 0, V7 39.
Flagged cover_short: V5 0, V7 48.

Only 5 scenarios are small beds, so the small-bed figures are a
warning sign rather than a measurement.
