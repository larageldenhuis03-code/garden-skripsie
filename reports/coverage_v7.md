## What V7 does

V7 is V5 with the cover floor switched off for beds under 6 m2. The
question it answers: does that keep V5's gain on medium and large beds
while removing the damage V5 did to small ones?

**Small beds.** V7's largest empty patch is 0.147, better than V5
(0.203) and level with V2 (0.147). With no floor and
the same seeds, a small bed under V7 should be built exactly as V2 builds
it. Checked scenario by scenario: all 5 identical to V2.

**Beds of 6 m2 or more.** V7's largest empty patch is 0.149, level with
V5 (0.149) and better than V2 (0.150). On these beds V7 and V5
apply the same rule with the same seeds. Checked: all 95 identical to V5.
So V7 is an exact splice - V2 on small beds, V5 on the rest - and its
figures are what that splice gives, not a new behaviour.

**Cost.** Median Z 0.9293 for V2, 0.9093 for V5, 0.9093 for V7.
Beds reaching their target: V2 33, V5 39, V7 39.
Flagged cover_short: V5 52, V7 48.

Only 5 scenarios are small beds, so the small-bed figures are a
warning sign rather than a measurement.
