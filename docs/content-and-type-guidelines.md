# Silly Goose MVP — content and typography guidelines

Updated: 2026-07-27

## Content voice

Write as a practical garden planner speaking to a Western Cape homeowner.

- Name the input, action or result.
- Prefer active voice and ordinary words.
- Keep one idea per paragraph.
- Use specific limitations instead of reassurance.
- Use sentence case for headings.
- Let a form question act as its own heading; do not repeat it as a small
  uppercase caption.
- Use “you” when it helps the user act.
- Keep claims proportionate to what the model and data can support.

Avoid:

- poetic contrast formulas such as “not X, but Y”;
- vague promises such as “designed to thrive”;
- AI-era filler such as “unlock”, “seamless”, “curated journey”, “reimagined”,
  “elevate” and “where X meets Y”;
- anthropomorphising the interface;
- three-part slogan stacks;
- decorative micro-headings that repeat the real heading; and
- unsupported precision, guarantees or scientific language.

## Typography

- Newsreader Variable is the display serif and botanical-name face.
- Instrument Sans Variable is the body and interface face.
- Body text starts at 17 px on desktop.
- Body line height is approximately 155%.
- Long-form text should stay within roughly 45–90 characters per line.
- The interface uses sentence case. All caps are reserved for data that
  conventionally needs it, not hierarchy.
- Use weight, size and whitespace before adding tracking or colour.
- Small text is functional metadata only and should not carry essential
  meaning.

## Research used

- [GOV.UK clear-language
  guidance](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/clear-language/)
  recommends plain English, active voice and short sentences and paragraphs.
- [GOV.UK heading
  guidance](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/headings/) recommends a
  consistent heading structure and sentence case.
- [GOV.UK form-label
  guidance](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/get-started/labels-legends-headings/)
  recommends making a single form question the heading, which avoids repeated
  captions.
- [Butterick’s body-text
  guidance](https://practicaltypography.com/body-text.html) prioritises font,
  size, line spacing and line length.
- [Butterick’s line-length
  guidance](https://practicaltypography.com/line-length.html) recommends an
  average of 45–90 characters.
- [Fontsource: Newsreader](https://fontsource.org/fonts/newsreader/install) and
  [Fontsource: Instrument
  Sans](https://fontsource.org/fonts/instrument-sans) provide the locally
  bundled variable fonts used by the product.
