# Silly Goose MVP — product specification

Status: implementation baseline  
Date: 2026-07-27

## Product promise

Silly Goose helps a Western Cape gardener turn a few observable garden
conditions into a short, legible list of indigenous plants worth considering.
It should feel closer to a small botanical field guide than a catalogue or
software dashboard.

## Primary user

A beginner or intermediate home gardener in the Western Cape who wants to plant
indigenous species but does not know where to start. The MVP assumes a desktop
planning session, with responsive support for smaller screens.

## Core journey

1. Enter a broad Western Cape area.
2. Describe sunlight, water availability and available growing space.
3. Choose one or more garden intentions.
4. Receive a ranked and explained recommendation set.
5. Open a plant profile and understand the evidence behind it.
6. Save plants to a device-local garden list.

## MVP surfaces

- Editorial landing view
- Five-part garden brief
- Ranked recommendation results
- Browseable 48-plant editorial collection
- Plant detail drawer
- Device-local saved garden
- Data and methodology explanation

## Functional acceptance criteria

- Every visible primary action has a working outcome.
- The garden brief changes the ranking and matching explanations.
- Results can be searched and filtered by plant form.
- A plant can be opened, saved and removed.
- Saved state survives a page refresh on the same device.
- The interface is keyboard accessible and remains usable at narrow widths.
- Plant facts distinguish source-derived measurements from editorial prototype
  attributes.

## Out of scope for this version

- Accounts and cross-device persistence
- Precise geolocation
- Nursery stock or pricing
- Drag-and-drop planting-bed design
- Automated legal enforcement
- Comprehensive soil chemistry or irrigation advice
- Production-grade synonym resolution

## Success question

Can a gardener describe their site in under two minutes and leave with a
credible, appealing shortlist they understand well enough to investigate
further?
